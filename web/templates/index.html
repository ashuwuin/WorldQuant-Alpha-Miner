WorldQuant BRAIN Documentation & FAQ

INSTITUTIONAL QUANT STANDARDS

Essential reference guide for simulation parameters, platform hurdles, self-correlation audits, and submission rules on WorldQuant BRAIN.

What are the minimum WorldQuant BRAIN submission hurdles? ▼
To qualify for submission, an alpha simulation must satisfy:
  • Sharpe Ratio: ≥ 1.25 (USA / EUR / ASI)
  • Fitness Score: ≥ 1.00 (Sharpe × sqrt(Returns / Turnover))
  • Turnover: 1.0% to 70.0%
  • Execution Margin: ≥ 10 bps (0.0010)
  • Self-Correlation: < 0.70 against all your active portfolio alphas
How does the background correlation auditor protect my submissions? ▼
Submitting an alpha with ≥ 0.70 correlation to an existing portfolio position wastes submission quota and results in platform rejection. Our background auditor queries /alphas/{id}/correlations in the background and tags every alpha with PASS or FAIL before you submit.
What is FastExpr vectorization vs ts_* operators? ▼
FastExpr operates on multidimensional stock × time matrices. Operators prefixed with ts_ compute time-series transforms (e.g. ts_delta, ts_rank, ts_backfill), while cross-sectional operators like rank() and group_neutralize() operate across all instruments at each timestamp.
Where are my credentials and alpha formulas stored? ▼
All formulas, backtest logs, and candidate records are stored locally in your private SQLite database (alphas_audit.db). Raw BRAIN credentials remain strictly in memory and are never committed or exposed.

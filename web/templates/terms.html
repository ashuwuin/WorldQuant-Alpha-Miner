RESEARCH GOVERNANCE

Operational mandate

Quantum Alpha Studio is an independent quantitative research acceleration gateway. All automated simulations, factor audits, and alpha submissions must strictly comply with WorldQuant BRAIN platform rules, code of conduct, and terms of service.

02
LOCAL SYSTEM BOUNDARY

Local compute & data sovereignty

The local research environment is operated on the user's machine. Users are responsible for managing their local environment, access credentials, and research data in accordance with applicable platform requirements.

03
SUBMISSION CONTROL

WorldQuant BRAIN consultant compliance

Users maintain complete control over which alphas are submitted to live portfolio evaluation. The auto-pilot pipeline generates and audits candidates against platform hurdles (Sharpe ≥ 1.25, Self-Correlation < 0.70, Turnover < 70%), but final cloud submission requires human confirmation or explicitly authorized consultant credentials.

04
RESEARCH-ONLY NOTICE

Liability & risk disclosure

All statistical metrics, backtest evaluations, Sharpe ratios, and fitness calculations generated within this platform are for academic, research, and algorithmic competition purposes only. Nothing within this software constitutes personalized investment advice or an offer of financial securities.

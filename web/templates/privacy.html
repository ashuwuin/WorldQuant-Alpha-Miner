Institutional Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Zero-Telemetry & Local Security Architecture

Quantum Alpha Studio is engineered as an institutional quantitative research portal. We respect the absolute proprietary nature of your trading strategies, mathematical formulas, and simulation workflows.

2. Credential Security & In-Memory Sessions

When authenticating with the WorldQuant BRAIN API:

  • Your WorldQuant BRAIN credentials are authenticated directly via TLS 1.3 with official endpoints (api.worldquantbrain.com).
  • Session tokens are stored exclusively in in-memory caches and secure, HTTP-only, SameSite local session cookies.
  • Your raw passwords and API tokens are never written to disk, never logged in public consoles, and never transmitted to third parties.

3. Local Strategy Vault Isolation

All quantitative FastExpr formulas, simulation results, factor scorecards, and cross-correlation matrix calculations are maintained strictly on your designated local environment in alphas_audit.db. No proprietary factor logic leaves your machine except when explicitly submitted by you to WorldQuant BRAIN.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

This application only uses strictly essential cookies required for session authentication state and user interface preferences (such as high-contrast display state). We do not employ third-party advertising, commercial tracking, or cross-site fingerprinting scripts.
